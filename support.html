Tidybun support

Quick answers are below. If yours is not here, email keytasupport@gmail.com and we will get back to you.

Common questions

My reminders are not arriving

First check that notifications are allowed for Tidybun in iOS Settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not hiding them. Then check Tidybun’s own settings for your reminder time and quiet hours.

Also note that iOS lets an app queue only a limited number of reminders (64 at a time). If you have many overdue items, opening Tidybun every so often tops the queue up again.

Why does it ask me to open the app now and then?

With “until done” reminders, iOS limits how many can be queued ahead of time. Opening the app, or marking something done, refills them. We cannot work around this limit.

How do I set up the widget?

Touch and hold your Home Screen, tap the plus button, search for Tidybun and pick a size. For the Lock Screen, touch and hold the lock screen, choose Customise, then add the widget. It shows what is overdue, and you can mark things done from it.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Tidybun’s settings and tap Restore Purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with. Nothing is charged again.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Tidybun Pro and tap Cancel. You keep Pro until the end of the period you already paid for. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual chores in the app. To remove everything, delete Tidybun from your iPhone. All your data is stored on the device, so that removes it. We keep no copy, so we cannot recover it afterwards.

Is the “Where do I start?” helper private?

Yes. Photos, text and voice are turned into steps on your iPhone, using Apple Vision and Apple Intelligence where available. Photos never leave the phone. One honest caveat: if your iPhone cannot do speech recognition on the device for your language, iOS may send the audio to Apple to be turned into text. You can type instead. See the privacy policy.

I see ads. How do I remove them?

Buy Remove Ads (one-time), or get Tidybun Pro or Pro Lifetime. The ads are small, non-personalised banners on two screens, and nothing tracks you.

Is Tidybun available in the EU?

No, not at the moment.
