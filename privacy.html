Tidybun privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Tidybun does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no servers run by the developer that receive your information, and no analytics. The one exception to know about is the banner ads, explained below.

This policy covers the Tidybun app for iPhone. “We” means the developer, Rosemarie Otero, who is the sole developer of Tidybun.

What stays on your iPhone

The names of your chores, notes, photos you attach, when you completed things, your settings and your bunny’s closet are stored on your device only. The app and its widget share this data through an App Group, which is a storage area on your iPhone that only our app and its widget can use. We never receive it, and we run no servers that could.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications created by the app on your iPhone. They do not go through any server. You can turn them off in iOS Settings at any time.

Photos, camera, microphone and speech

These are used only by the “Where do I start?” helper and by attaching a photo to a chore, and only if you choose to use them. Tidybun asks for permission first, and you can say no and still use the rest of the app.

  • Photos and camera. Photos are analysed on your device using Apple’s Vision framework. They are not uploaded by Tidybun.
  • Turning your input into steps. Typed text, voice and photo descriptions are turned into small steps on your device, using Apple Intelligence where your iPhone supports it, or simpler on-device rules where it does not. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Voice. Tidybun uses Apple’s on-device speech recognition when your iPhone supports it for your language. If it does not, iOS may send the audio to Apple’s speech service to be turned into text. That is handled by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy, not by us, and we never receive the audio.

Purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). Apple processes your payment and tells the app whether you own a purchase. We do not see your name, card or payment details, and we do not receive any record of your purchases.

Ads, Google AdMob and consent

The free version shows a banner ad from Google AdMob on two screens. Paying to remove ads, or subscribing to Pro or buying Pro Lifetime, removes them.

  • Ads are non-personalised. They are not based on your interests or activity in other apps.
  • Tidybun does not track you across other companies’ apps and websites. It does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not show the App Tracking Transparency prompt.
  • Where required, Google’s consent message (the User Messaging Platform) asks for your choice before ads load. You can change it later where the app offers that option.

Google’s software in the app collects some data itself in order to serve and measure ads and keep them secure. Based on Google’s disclosures, this includes:

  • a device or app identifier that is not the advertising ID, used for things like limiting how often an ad is shown and detecting fraud;
  • ad impressions and taps;
  • diagnostic information such as performance and crash data;
  • approximate location, worked out from your IP address.

None of this is linked to your identity by us, and none of it is used for tracking. This is the data we declare in our App Store privacy label. Google acts on its own account for this data. Read Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Children

Tidybun is rated 4+ because it has no objectionable content, but it is not directed at children and is not made for them. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because we collect nothing, there is nothing to delete, but if you believe a child has sent us information, write to us.

How long data is kept, and deleting it

Your data lives on your iPhone until you remove it. You can delete a chore in the app, or delete everything by deleting Tidybun, which removes the app, its widget and its stored data from your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot restore it for you either. Any iCloud or device backup you make is controlled by you through Apple.

Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you, there is generally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you are in a place with privacy rights and want to ask us anything, email keytasupport@gmail.com. For data Google or Apple hold, contact them using their own tools.

Where Tidybun is available

Tidybun is not available in the European Union.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will change the date at the top. If a change affects what is collected, we will say so in the app update notes.

Contact
