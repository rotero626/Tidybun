A gentle nudge for the chores you keep forgetting

Tidybun remembers when you last did each thing, shows you only what is overdue, and a friendly dust bunny reminds you every day until it is done.

How it works

  1. Add the things that come round again: bins, bedding, the filter in the dishwasher, the vet.
  2. Say how often each one should happen. Tidybun counts from when you last did it, not from a fixed date.
  3. Open the app to see only what is overdue. Tap Done, and the clock starts again.

Missed a week? That is fine. Nothing is lost and nobody is scolding you.

What you get

Only what is overdue

No long lists of everything you might do one day. Just what needs doing now.

A bunny who nudges kindly

A dust bunny reminds you once a day until the job is done, then leaves you alone.

Where do I start?

Stuck on a messy room? Share a photo, type it out or say it aloud, and Tidybun turns it into a few tiny first steps. It works on your phone, and your photos never leave it.

Rewards you earn

Finish chores and your bunny unlocks things for its closet. They are earned, never sold.

A widget

See what is overdue, and mark it done, from your Home Screen or Lock Screen.

Private by design

No account, no sign-up, no analytics. Your chores stay on your iPhone.

Free, with optional extras

The core of Tidybun is free and shows a small, non-personalised banner ad on two screens. You can remove the ads with a one-time purchase, or choose Tidybun Pro for extras such as unlimited items. See the subscription terms and the privacy policy for the details.
